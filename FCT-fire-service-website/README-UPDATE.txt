FCT Fire Service — Banner Video Update (v2)

How to use:
1. Unzip this archive.
2. Copy/replace into your existing firefront-html project:
   - index.html
   - css/styles.css
   - js/main.js
   - assets/videos/fct-fire-service.mp4  (create folders if needed)

Video controls:
- Native play/pause, progress bar, volume, fullscreen
- Double-tap (or double-click) LEFT side  → skip back 10 seconds
- Double-tap (or double-click) RIGHT side → skip forward 10 seconds
- Arrow Left / Arrow Right keys when video is focused
- Close: × button, backdrop click, or Escape

Only the banner section was modified. Hero and all other sections are unchanged.
